GRAMMAR TIME ⭐

Did you notice these words?

Our story used was and were to talk about things that happened in the past.

WAS
I · He · She · It

Use was with one person or thing.

🐻 Ben was happy.
WERE
You · We · They

Use were with "we," "you," "they," and plural nouns.

🐦 The birds were in the trees.
💡 Think about time!

☀️ Today: Ben is at the park.
🌙 Yesterday: Ben was at the park.

☀️ Today: They are happy.
🌙 Yesterday: They were happy.
